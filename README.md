Accelerated Mineralisation Reactor (AMR)
Overview

The Accelerated Mineralisation Reactor (AMR) is an innovative solution aimed at reducing carbon emissions from the power and steel sectors. It captures CO₂ from industrial flue gases and reacts it with steel slag (an industrial by-product) to form stable carbonates. These carbonates can then be used to produce sustainable construction materials such as green cement.

This approach not only reduces greenhouse gas emissions but also converts industrial waste into valuable resources.

Problem Statement
The power and steel industries are among the largest contributors to global CO₂ emissions.
Steel production generates large amounts of slag waste.
Conventional carbon capture methods are expensive and lack value generation.
Proposed Solution

The AMR system integrates carbon capture, waste utilization, and material production into a single process:

Capture CO₂ from industrial exhaust gases
React captured CO₂ with steel slag
Form stable carbonate compounds
Convert the output into green construction materials
Process Flow
Capture CO2 → React with Slag → Form Carbonates → Produce Green Materials
